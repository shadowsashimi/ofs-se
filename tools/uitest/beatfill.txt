# Beat fill and depth from loudness, against audio whose clicks get louder
# over the minute. Detection marks the quiet opening as having no music and
# makes a 120 BPM chapter of the rest; the fill writes a stroke every two beats
# across that chapter, saving the result as a pattern stamps it back, and
# loudness scales the quieter strokes down.
# Special functions at 1600x900 after Beat fill is picked: the tool bar's
# second row is y 291 (Beat fill 697, Loudness 902), Fill 799,679, save as a
# pattern 799,711, the pattern list 799,409. Loudness has Apply at 799,569.
#! media ramp
#! covers src/UI/OFS_SpecialFunctions.* src/state/SpecialFunctionsState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
expect audio_only 1

note measure the tempo, which is what the fill writes against
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 238
wait 20
click 800 349
waituntil chapters >= 1 900
state 01-detected
note the quiet opening is marked as having no music, so the music starts at 15s
expect chapters 2
click 1088 313
wait 10
note the playhead goes into the music, which is the chapter the fill works on
seek 30
wait 10

note open special functions and pick the beat fill
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 190
wait 15
click 697 291
wait 10
shot 02-beatfill
windows 02-beatfill
expect actions 0

note fill the chapter: a point every quarter note is one every half second
click 799 679
wait 20
state 03-filled
shot 03-filled
waituntil actions >= 80 5
note the strokes alternate between the bottom and top asked for
expect first_position 10

note save what was filled as a pattern, then stamp it back over the chapter
key ctrl+a
wait 5
click 799 711
wait 10
type bar
wait 5
click 715 711
wait 15
shot 04-pattern-saved
state 04-pattern-saved
click 799 679
wait 20
state 05-stamped
waituntil actions >= 80 5

note loudness: the quiet start is scaled in, the loud end keeps its depth
click 902 291
wait 10
key ctrl+a
wait 5
state 06-before-loudness
expect first_position 10
expect last_position 90
click 799 569
wait 20
state 07-loudness
shot 07-loudness
note the first stroke is pulled towards the middle, the last one is not
waituntil first_position >= 20 5
expect last_position 90
quit
