# Making and naming chapters by hand in the chapters panel. With one chapter
# at 1600x900 its row is at y 427: name field from x 538 to 912, row menu
# button at 1067.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ChapterManager.* OFS-lib/state/states/ChapterState.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note open chapters from the View menu
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 238
wait 20
expect chapters 0

note create a chapter at the playhead with its keybinding's action
action create_chapter
wait 10
state 01-created
shot 01-created
expect chapters 1
note a chapter made by hand has a name to start from
expect chapter_name Chapter 1

note rename it: click the name, select what is there, type over it
click 730 427
wait 5
key ctrl+a
wait 3
type Intro
wait 5
key enter
wait 5
state 02-renamed
shot 02-renamed
expect chapter_name Intro

note the row menu
click 1067 427
wait 10
shot 03-row-menu
windows 03-row-menu
quit
