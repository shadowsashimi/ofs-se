# The 3D simulator's top and side views: two small views beside the model,
# from straight above and from the side, moving with it. Off to begin with.
# With the pose handles on they carry handles too, so a pose can be set from
# above or the side. Add > SR6 with twist is at 30 694, then 41 603, at
# 1600x900.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:drawInsetViews src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:insetLayout
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 603
wait 20
expect script_axes stroke,surge,sway,twist,roll,pitch
expect inset_views 0
action toggle_simulator_views
wait 40
expect inset_views 1
shot 01-views

note leaning and slid, which the top view shows as a move across the floor
simaxis stroke 60
simaxis sway 90
simaxis surge 80
simaxis roll 80
simaxis pitch 75
simaxis twist 70
wait 60
expect inset_views 1
shot 02-posed
simaxis stroke -1
simaxis sway -1
simaxis surge -1
simaxis roll -1
simaxis pitch -1
simaxis twist -1
wait 10

note the pose handles, in the views as well as on the model
action toggle_pose_handles
wait 10
expect pose_shown 1
shot 03-handles

note slide the floor square in the top view: sway and surge
insethandle top floor
wait 5
press left
wait 3
insethandle top floor 12 0
wait 3
insethandle top floor 12 -12
wait 5
shot 04-top-floor-drag
waituntil pose_value:sway >= 0 5
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.sway 1
expect actions:uitest.surge 1

note tilt the tip in the side view: pitch
insethandle side tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
insethandle side tip 10 0
wait 3
insethandle side tip 10 0
wait 5
shot 05-side-tip-drag
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.pitch 1

note and off again
action toggle_simulator_views
wait 10
expect inset_views 0
quit
