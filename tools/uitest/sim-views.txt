# The 3D simulator's views: one, as usual, or two in its place, from straight
# above and from the side or the front. With the pose handles on, each view
# has the handles that read best in it and no others: from above the floor
# square, the ring and the tip, whose knob is set a little aside from the
# floor square's; from the side or front the case. Add > SR6 with twist is at
# 30 694, then 41 603, at 1600x900.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:drawInsetViews src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:insetLayout
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 603
wait 20
expect script_axes stroke,surge,sway,twist,roll,pitch
expect sim_views 0
expect inset_views 0
action toggle_simulator_views
wait 40
expect sim_views 1
expect inset_views 1
shot 01-top-side

note leaning and slid
simaxis stroke 60
simaxis sway 90
simaxis surge 80
simaxis roll 80
simaxis pitch 75
simaxis twist 70
wait 60
shot 02-posed
simaxis stroke -1
simaxis sway -1
simaxis surge -1
simaxis roll -1
simaxis pitch -1
simaxis twist -1
wait 10

note the pose handles, each in its own view
action toggle_pose_handles
wait 10
expect pose_shown 1
shot 03-handles

note the floor square from above: sway and surge
insethandle top floor
wait 5
press left
wait 3
insethandle top floor 8 0
wait 3
insethandle top floor 8 -8
wait 5
shot 04-top-floor-drag
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.sway 1
expect actions:uitest.surge 1

note the tip from above, by its knob set aside: roll and pitch
insethandle top tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
insethandle top tip 8 0
wait 3
insethandle top tip 8 -8
wait 5
shot 05-top-tip-drag
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.roll 1
expect actions:uitest.pitch 1

note the case from the side: the stroke
insethandle side case
wait 5
press left
wait 3
insethandle side case 0 -12
wait 3
insethandle side case 0 -12
wait 5
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest 1

note top and front, then back to one view
action toggle_simulator_views
wait 40
expect sim_views 2
shot 06-top-front
action toggle_simulator_views
wait 10
expect sim_views 0
expect inset_views 0
quit
