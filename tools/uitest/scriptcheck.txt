# The script check finds strokes a device cannot follow, and slows them down.
# A beat fill at a 32nd note writes strokes far over any device's speed, which
# is what the check is then pointed at.
# Special functions at 1600x900: Beat fill 697,291, the note length list
# 799,465 with 1/32 its eighth row at 799,663, Fill 799,679.
# Script check at 1600x900, which opens 540x612 in the middle: presets
# Gentle 626,249, Typical 799,249, Fast 972,249; Check 799,455; Slow the fast
# ones down 641,547.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptCheck.* src/state/ScriptCheckState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note two pairs a couple of frames apart, 80 units apart: far too fast, and
note closer together than the gentle preset's 100ms
hold shift
lanemove 3.00 10
tap
lanemove 3.067 90
tap
lanemove 3.30 10
tap
lanemove 3.367 90
tap
let shift
wait 10
expect actions 4

note open the script check from the View menu
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 260
wait 20
expect show_script_check 1
shot 01-open
windows 01-open

note check: the pairs are both too close together and too fast
click 799 455
wait 20
state 02-checked
shot 02-checked
waituntil check_problems >= 2 10

note slowing them down shortens the strokes and leaves their timing alone
click 641 547
wait 20
state 03-slowed
shot 03-slowed
expect actions 4
expect check_problems 0
expect first_position 10
expect last_position 36
quit
