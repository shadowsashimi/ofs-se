# A video whose name has dots of its own, as downloads often do. The axis of a
# script is the last dot separated part of its name only when that part is an
# axis, so the main script stays the stroke rather than an axis called
# "COM - clip", and the scripts added beside it are still roll and pitch.
#! covers OFS-lib/Funscript/Funscript.* src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.* src/UI/OFS_SpecialFunctions.*
#! media dotted
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
expect script_axes stroke

note add roll
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 200
wait 30

note add pitch
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 222
wait 30
state 01-scripts
expect scripts 3
expect script_axes stroke,roll,pitch

note the 3D view counts all three as driven
click 1560 43
wait 20
