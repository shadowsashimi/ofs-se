# Posing the 3D model with handles (experimental): the case for stroke, the
# ring for twist, the tip for roll and pitch, the floor square for surge and
# sway. Letting go of a drag writes a point at the playhead on each axis that
# moved, as one undo. Handles for axes with no track do nothing, and the
# handles are hidden while playing. Add > OSR2 with twist is at 30 694, then
# 41 555, at 1600x900.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/state/SimulatorState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note twist, roll and pitch tracks, but no surge or sway
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 555
wait 20
expect script_axes stroke,twist,roll,pitch

note off by default, and on from its setting
expect pose_shown 0
action toggle_pose_handles
wait 10
expect pose_shown 1
shot 01-handles

note drag the case up its length. The empty main track holds the model at
note stroke 0, as the simulator always has, so the point lands above that
posehandle case
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle case 0 -15
wait 3
posehandle case 0 -15
wait 3
posehandle case 0 -15
wait 3
shot 02-stroke-dragging
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest 1
waituntil first_position >= 20 5

note push the tip to the side: a roll point, and pitch left alone if it did not move
posehandle tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 20 0
wait 3
posehandle tip 20 0
wait 3
shot 03-tilt-dragging
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.roll 1
note seen from the front, pitch moves the tip towards the viewer, so a sideways drag leaves it alone
expect actions:uitest.pitch 0

note and up and down is pitch instead, up being away from the viewer
posehandle tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 0 -20
wait 3
posehandle tip 0 -20
wait 3
shot 03b-pitch-end-on
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.pitch 1

note one undo takes the last pose, the pitch point, back out
key ctrl+z
wait 5
expect actions:uitest.pitch 0
expect actions:uitest.roll 1
key ctrl+y
wait 5
expect actions:uitest.pitch 1

note the ring is edge on from straight in front, so it is dragged across:
note right turns the front of the case to the right, raising twist
posehandle ring
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle ring 18 0
wait 3
posehandle ring 18 0
wait 3
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.twist 1
shot 04-posed
lanehead 1 name
tap
wait 10
expect active_title uitest.twist
waituntil first_position >= 55 5
lanehead 0 name
tap
wait 10

note seen from above, the ring is followed round instead: Alt and drag
note up raises the camera 0.3 degrees a pixel
hold alt
drag 1092 420 1092 300 10
let alt
wait 10
waituntil camera_elevation >= 25 5
seek 6
wait 10
posehandle ring
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle ring 15 0
wait 3
posehandle ring 15 0
wait 3
shot 05-ring-from-above
release left
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.twist 2

note the floor square has no surge or sway track to write, so it does nothing
posehandle floor
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle floor 20 0
wait 3
expect pose_dragging -1
release left
wait 10
expect scripts 4

note the handles are hidden while playing
play
wait 20
expect pose_shown 0
pause
wait 10
expect pose_shown 1
quit
