# Beat ticks under the waveform, and the bass only waveform. Run against
# audio, whose waveform is made as soon as it opens: the test media is a click
# every half second, so there is a hit to find twice a second.
# Preferences at 1600x900: Options 327,13 with Preferences at 380,86; the
# sidebar's Timeline section 460,356; after scrolling to the bottom of it,
# Bass only 1026,589.
#! media mp3
#! covers OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Waveform.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note the waveform comes on its own for audio, and the ticks are found from it
waituntil waveform_shown >= 1 600
wait 20
state 01-full
shot 01-full
expect waveform_bass 0
note a click every half second for a minute, so about 120 of them
waituntil beat_ticks >= 100 20

note switch to the bass only waveform, which reads the audio again
click 327 13
wait 5
click 380 86
wait 20
expect preferences_open 1
click 460 356
wait 20
shot 02-timeline-section
note the waveform settings are at the bottom of the section
move 880 600
wheel -10
wait 10
shot 03-waveform-settings
windows 03-waveform-settings

note bass only reads the audio again, and the ticks are found from that instead
click 1026 589
wait 10
expect waveform_bass 1
waituntil waveform_shown >= 1 900
wait 30
state 04-bass
shot 04-bass
waituntil beat_ticks >= 50 60
quit
