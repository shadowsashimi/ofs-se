# Machine presets. Add > a machine in the Tracks panel adds the tracks it has
# that the project lacks, and sets the Devices panel to send to its axes and
# no others. In the Add menu at 1600x900: SSR1 41 507, OSR2 41 531, OSR2 with
# twist 41 555, SR6 41 579, SR6 with twist 41 603.
#! covers src/api/OFS_TCodeMachines.h src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.cpp:UseMachine src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.cpp:CurrentMachine src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:AddMachineTracks src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:ShowTracksWindow
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
expect scripts 1
note every axis is on to begin with, which is no machine in particular
expect device_machine -1

note Add > OSR2: roll and pitch tracks, and only stroke, roll and pitch sent
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 531
wait 20
expect scripts 3
expect script_axes stroke,roll,pitch
expect device_machine 1

note Add > SR6 with twist on top of it adds only what is missing
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 603
wait 20
expect scripts 6
expect script_axes stroke,roll,pitch,surge,sway,twist
expect device_machine 4
state 01-sr6
shot 01-sr6

note picking a machine with fewer axes adds nothing and turns the extra axes off
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 507
wait 20
expect scripts 6
expect device_machine 0
quit
