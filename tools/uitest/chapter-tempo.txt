# A chapter's tempo can be counted half or double time, and keeps that when
# measured again. The test media is a click every half second for a minute,
# which detection makes one chapter of at 120 BPM.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ChapterManager.* OFS-lib/state/states/ChapterState.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Waveform.* src/UI/OFS_ScriptPositionsOverlays.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note open chapters from the View menu, and detect
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 238
wait 20
click 800 349
wait 600
expect chapter 120,0.0,60.0

note the chapter's menu: Double time (x2) 1147,596, Half time (/2) 1134,620
click 1067 468
wait 10
shot 01-row-menu
windows 01-row-menu

note half time: 120 becomes 60
click 1134 620
wait 10
state 02-half
expect chapter 60,0.0,60.0

note measuring again keeps the correction rather than going back to 120
click 800 349
wait 60
state 03-measured-again
expect chapter 60,0.0,60.0

note the tempo grid's settings in the Mode panel follow the chapter, and double it there
click 786 43
wait 10
shot 04-tempo-settings
move 1450 200
wheel -1
wait 10
click 1515 98
wait 10
state 05-doubled-in-mode-panel
shot 05-doubled-in-mode-panel
expect chapter 120,0.0,60.0

note double again from the chapter's menu, and it holds through measuring
click 1067 468
wait 10
click 1147 596
wait 10
expect chapter 240,0.0,60.0
click 800 349
wait 60
state 06-240-measured
expect chapter 240,0.0,60.0
quit
