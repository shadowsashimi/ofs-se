# A chapter's tempo can be set by hand, and measuring every chapter leaves it
# alone. The test media is a click every half second for a minute, which
# detection makes one chapter of at 120 BPM.
# Chapters: Measure all 800,349, the chapter's tempo 978,468, its menu 1067,468.
# Toolbar Tempo 786,43; Mode panel BPM +1 1560,220.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ChapterManager.* OFS-lib/state/states/ChapterState.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Waveform.* src/UI/OFS_ScriptPositionsOverlays.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note open chapters from the View menu, and detect
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 238
wait 20
click 800 349
wait 600
expect chapter 120,0.0,60.0

note the tempo grid follows the chapter; its BPM can be changed there, which sets it by hand
click 786 43
wait 10
click 1560 220
wait 10
state 01-typed
shot 01-typed
expect chapter 121,0.0,60.0

note measuring all chapters leaves a tempo set by hand alone
click 800 349
wait 60
state 02-measured-all
shot 02-measured-all
expect chapter 121,0.0,60.0

note measuring the chapter itself asks for the audio's answer
click 978 468
wait 60
state 03-measured-one
expect chapter 120,0.0,60.0

note the chapter's menu, with its typed tempo (field 1145,672, Keep this tempo 1139,700)
click 1067 468
wait 10
shot 04-row-menu
windows 04-row-menu

note type a tempo there
click 1145 672
wait 5
key ctrl+a
type 128
key enter
wait 10
key escape
wait 10
state 05-typed-in-menu
expect chapter 128,0.0,60.0

note untick Keep this tempo, and measuring all chapters measures it again
click 1067 468
wait 10
click 1139 700
wait 10
click 800 349
wait 60
state 06-unkept
expect chapter 120,0.0,60.0
quit
