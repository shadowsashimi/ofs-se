# Moving and resizing the simulator by its frame, in 2D and 3D, and turning
# the 3D camera with Alt and drag.
# Simulator panel at 1600x900: Lock 1360,579. Toolbar: 2D 1489, 3D 1560 (y 43).
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/UI/OFS_VulvaHeightMap.h src/state/SimulatorState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
wait 10

note unlock it
click 1360 579
wait 10
expect sim_locked 0
state 01-2d
shot 01-2d

note 3D
click 1560 43
wait 60
state 02-3d
shot 02-3d

note hovering the model shows its frame
move 595 450
wait 5
shot 03-3d-hover

note dragging inside the frame moves it
drag 595 450 695 450 10
wait 10
state 04-3d-moved
expect sim_p1 695,171
expect sim_p2 695,531

note the frame is the most the model can take up, so it does not change as it moves
note the frame takes in the floor and wall around the model as well
expect sim_frame 496,61,894,689

note dragging the left edge of the frame out grows it, the right edge staying put:
note scaled by 438/398 about the middle of the frame's right edge at 894,377
drag 496 377 456 377 10
wait 20
state 05-3d-resized
shot 05-3d-resized
expect sim_p1 675,150
expect sim_p2 675,547

note Alt and drag over the model turns the camera, 0.4 degrees a pixel
hold alt
drag 675 400 600 400 10
let alt
wait 20
state 06-3d-turned
shot 06-3d-turned
expect camera_yaw 30
expect camera_elevation 0
expect sim_p1 675,150

note back to 2D: the top end of the bar resizes it
click 1489 43
wait 20
move 675 158
wait 5
shot 07-2d-hover-end
quit
