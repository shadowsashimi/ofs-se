# TCode to a machine on wifi, against a stand-in that logs what arrives, so
# the commands OFS sent are in mock-tcode.log beside the results. A real OSR is
# the thing to try before trusting it; this shows that OFS says the right
# words, at the right rate, on the right axes.
# Devices panel at 1600x900: View 266,13 with Devices at 300,282. Backends
# Intiface 552,260, Serial 793,260, Wifi 1034,260. On wifi: address 792,363,
# port 792,419, UDP 613,451, TCP 973,451, send every 792,513, Connect 792,551.
# Project > Add > Shortcuts: menu 89,13, submenu row 425,178 adds twist; the
# second lane's header is at 91,739. In the axis table, the stroke row's
# Highest is at 663,673.
#! server mock-tcode
#! covers src/api/OFS_TCode.* src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.* src/state/DeviceLinkState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
note the window shows five seconds around the playhead, so it sits where the
note strokes are going to be placed
seek 6.5
wait 10

note four strokes half a second apart, for the machine to follow
hold shift
lanemove 6.0 10
tap
lanemove 6.5 90
tap
lanemove 7.0 10
tap
lanemove 7.5 90
tap
let shift
wait 10
expect actions 4

note a twist script too, and made active, so more than one axis is driven
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 178
wait 30
expect scripts 2
note its lane header makes it the one points land in
click 91 739
wait 10
expect active_title uitest.twist
hold shift
lanemove 6.2 20
tap
lanemove 6.8 80
tap
let shift
wait 10

note open the devices panel and pick the wifi machine
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 282
wait 20
expect show_devices 1
click 1034 260
wait 10
expect device_status 0

note point it at the stand-in, which listens on 127.0.0.1:8000
dclick 792 363
wait 3
key ctrl+a
type 127.0.0.1
key enter
wait 5
shot 01-wifi
windows 01-wifi

note connecting opens the socket; nothing has to answer for UDP. The first
note connect asks first, with I understand at 645,593 (see devices.txt)
click 792 551
wait 10
expect device_safety_note 1
expect device_status 0
click 645 593
wait 20
expect device_safety_note 0
state 02-connected
shot 02-connected
expect device_status 2

note playing sends a line about every ten milliseconds, with only what moved
seek 5.8
play
wait 240
pause
wait 30
state 03-played
waituntil device_moves >= 20 10

note the stroke and the twist both moved, each on its own channel
state 03-channels
expect device_channels L0,R0

note past the end of the script nothing drives the axes, so they ease home:
note two seconds of holding, then the ease itself, and frames are not seconds
seek 20
waituntil device_axes_home >= 1 900
state 04-homed
shot 04-homed
expect device_axes L0:500,L1:500,L2:500,R0:500,R1:500,R2:500,A1:500

note the stroke axis limited to half the machine's travel: the script is
note mapped into what is left, so its 90 arrives as 450 of 999 rather than 899
dclick 663 673
wait 3
key ctrl+a
type 50
key enter
wait 10
seek 6.5
wait 60
state 05-limited
shot 05-limited
waituntil device_stroke <= 460 30
waituntil device_stroke >= 440 30

note disconnecting puts every axis back to its rest position
click 792 551
wait 20
state 04-disconnected
expect device_status 0
quit
