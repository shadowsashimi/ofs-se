# Driving a device from the script, against a stand-in for Intiface Central:
# it answers the handshake, offers one device that can be told a position, and
# logs every message, so what OFS sent is in intiface.log beside the results.
# Real hardware is the thing to try before trusting it; this only shows that
# OFS says the right words in the right order.
# Devices panel at 1600x900: View 266,13 with Devices at 300,282. The backend
# bar is at y 260, Intiface 552; Connect is at 792,457.
#! server mock-intiface
#! covers src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.* src/state/DeviceLinkState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
note the window shows five seconds around the playhead, so it sits where the
note strokes are going to be placed
seek 6.5
wait 10

note four strokes half a second apart, for it to follow
hold shift
lanemove 6.0 10
tap
lanemove 6.5 90
tap
lanemove 7.0 10
tap
lanemove 7.5 90
tap
let shift
wait 10
expect actions 4

note open the devices panel
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 282
wait 20
expect show_devices 1
expect device_status 0
shot 01-panel
windows 01-panel

note connect: the handshake, then the device list
click 792 457
waituntil device_status >= 2 300
state 02-connected
shot 02-connected
expect device_status 2
expect device_count 1

note playing sends one move per stroke, not one per frame
seek 5.5
play
wait 180
pause
wait 10
state 03-played
waituntil device_moves >= 4 10
waituntil device_moves <= 12 10
quit
