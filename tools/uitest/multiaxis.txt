# The 3D simulator driven by more than one axis. Twist and roll scripts are
# added from Project > Add > Shortcuts (submenu rows at x 425: surge 90,
# sway 112, suck 134, twist 156, roll 178, pitch 200), and each gets a stroke.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/UI/OFS_VulvaHeightMap.h src/state/SimulatorState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note a stroke on the main script
hold shift
lanemove 4.0 10
tap
lanemove 5.0 90
tap
lanemove 6.0 10
tap
let shift
wait 5

note add twist
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 156
wait 30

note add roll
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 178
wait 30
state 01-three-scripts
expect scripts 3

note the 3D view, with the playhead in the middle of the stroke
click 1560 43
wait 10
seek 4.6
wait 20
shot 02-3d-three-scripts
windows 02-3d-three-scripts
quit
