# The player controls and the timeline's zoom, with the mouse.
# At 1600x900 everything that drives playback is one row along y 812:
# previous frame 26, back 3 s 73, play 118, forward 3 s 164, next frame 211,
# the time, then -10% at 545, the speed field 604, +10% 671, 1x 718, mute
# 775 and the volume slider from 800 to the edge; the seek bar along y 853
# from x 12 to 1588.
#! covers OFS-lib/UI/OFS_VideoplayerControls.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Videopreview.* OFS-lib/UI/GradientBar.* OFS-lib/Funscript/FunscriptHeatmap.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.* OFS-lib/videoplayer/* OFS-lib/OFS_MpvLoader.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
expect player_time 5.000

note next frame, then previous frame
click 211 811
wait 10
expect player_time 5.033
click 26 811
wait 10
expect player_time 5.000

note forward three seconds
click 164 811
wait 15
expect player_time 8.000

note speed up by a tenth, then back to normal
click 671 812
wait 5
expect speed 1.10
click 718 812
wait 5
expect speed 1.00

note type a speed into the field and press Enter
dclick 604 812
wait 5
key ctrl+a
type 0.5
key enter
wait 5
expect speed 0.50
click 718 812
wait 5
expect speed 1.00

note click the middle of the seek bar
click 800 853
wait 20
state 01-seek-bar
shot 01-seek-bar
note halfway along the bar, now the whole width, is half the minute
expect player_time 30.012

note the mouse wheel over the timeline zooms it; aimed at the playhead, which
note the seek bar click left at about 30 s
lanemove 30.0 50
wait 5
state 02-before-zoom
wheel 3
wait 30
state 03-zoomed-in
note each notch zooms by a tenth: three in from 5 s
note the zoom eases in over 150ms, so it is waited for rather than counted in frames
waituntil visible_time <= 3.55 120
waituntil visible_time >= 3.45 120
wheel -6
wait 30
state 04-zoomed-out
note and six out from there
waituntil visible_time >= 5.55 120
waituntil visible_time <= 5.65 120
shot 04-zoomed-out
quit
