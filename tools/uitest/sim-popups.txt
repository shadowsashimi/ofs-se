# The simulator stays on screen while a popup is open, and popups are drawn
# over it. Simulator panel at 1600x900: Lock 1360,579. Toolbar 3D 1560,43.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/UI/OFS_VulvaHeightMap.h src/state/SimulatorState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
wait 10

note the timeline's right click menu, with the 2D simulator still showing
click 800 720 right
wait 10
state 01-context-2d
shot 01-context-2d
expect open_popups 1
expect sim_drawn 1
key escape
wait 10
expect open_popups 0

note and with the 3D view
click 1560 43
wait 20
click 800 720 right
wait 10
state 02-context-3d
shot 02-context-3d
expect open_popups 1
expect sim_drawn 1
key escape
wait 10

note a menu opened over the simulator is drawn on top of it
click 1360 579
wait 10
drag 595 400 145 400 10
wait 10
click 28 13
wait 10
state 03-menu-over
shot 03-menu-over
expect open_popups 1
expect sim_drawn 1
key escape
wait 10
