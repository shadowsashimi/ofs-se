# The waveform of one channel, for binaural audio with the stroking sounds on
# one side and voices on the other, and snapping points onto the beat ticks.
# The test media clicks every half second on the left and hums, swelling
# slowly, on the right. Preferences at 1600x900: Options 327,13, Preferences
# 380,86, the Timeline section 460,339; scrolled to its bottom, Channel Both
# 681,573, Left 878,573, Right 1075,573, and its close button 1178,224.
# Snap to beats is on the toolbar at 1076,45.
#! media stereo-split
#! covers OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Waveform.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.* OFS-lib/state/states/BaseOverlayState.h
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
waituntil waveform_shown >= 1 600
wait 20
expect waveform_channel 0
shot 01-both

note the left channel alone: the clicks, two a second for twenty seconds
