# The simulator can be grabbed wherever its frame is, not only over the
# video, and can be fitted to the video player.
# Simulator panel at 1600x900: Lock 1360,591, Fit to video checkbox 1334,622.
# Toolbar: 2D 1489, 3D 1560 (y 43).
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.* src/UI/OFS_VulvaHeightMap.h src/state/SimulatorState.h OFS-lib/videoplayer/OFS_VideoplayerWindow.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
wait 10

note unlock it
click 1360 591
wait 10
expect sim_locked 0

note move the bar down until its middle is over the timeline
drag 595 350 595 650 10
wait 10
state 01-over-timeline
shot 01-over-timeline
expect sim_p1 595,471

note grab it over the timeline, where it could not be grabbed before, and bring it back
drag 595 700 595 400 10
wait 10
state 02-back
expect sim_p1 595,171

note fit to video, against the right side
click 1334 622
wait 10
expect sim_fit 1
state 03-fit-2d
shot 03-fit-2d
note against the right edge of the picture, x 1161, not of the player's window
expect sim_p1 1072,122
expect sim_p2 1072,580

note 3D is fitted too, floor and wall included, to the player's window rather
note than the picture: it is a scene of its own, not laid over the video
click 1560 43
wait 30
state 04-fit-3d
shot 04-fit-3d
expect sim_frame 990,123,1278,577

note moving it by hand, from the middle of its frame, turns fitting off
drag 1132 350 1032 350 10
wait 10
state 05-by-hand
expect sim_fit 0
expect sim_p1 1034,201
quit
