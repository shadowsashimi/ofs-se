# Special functions change a selection when their value is changed.
# Special functions window at 1600x900: the tool bar is two rows, Range
# extend 663,243, Simplify 799,243, Multi-axis 935,243, Beat fill 697,274 and
# Loudness 902,274. The value row is at y 336, Extend by's +5 at 988 and the
# typed field around 799.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_SpecialFunctions.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note a zigzag between 30 and 70, enough points for the tools
hold shift
lanemove 3.0 30
tap
lanemove 3.5 70
tap
lanemove 4.0 30
tap
lanemove 4.5 70
tap
lanemove 5.0 30
tap
lanemove 5.5 70
tap
lanemove 6.0 30
tap
let shift
wait 5
expect actions 7
expect positions 30,70,30,70,30,70,30

note open special functions and select everything
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 190
wait 15
key ctrl+a
wait 5
expect selection 7
shot 01-range-extend
windows 01-range-extend

note five presses of +5: the strokes spread out by 25
click 988 336
wait 3
click 988 336
wait 3
click 988 336
wait 3
click 988 336
wait 3
click 988 336
wait 10
state 02-extended
shot 02-extended
note the strokes are stretched towards the ends, and the selection is still there
expect positions 30,95,5,95,5,95,30
expect selection 7

note Simplify, on the same selection, which is still selected
click 799 243
wait 10
shot 03-simplify
state 03-simplify
expect selection 7

note type a tolerance: points that add little to the shape go
dclick 799 336
wait 3
key ctrl+a
type 0.3
key enter
wait 10
state 04-simplified
shot 04-simplified
note the zigzag is reduced to its outline, and the points left are still selected
expect actions 3
expect positions 30,95,30
expect selection 3
quit
