# A trackpad scrolls in small fractions of a notch. Over the lanes they zoom
# smoothly, and two fingers sideways move through time, as Shift and a mouse
# wheel do. A whole notch from a mouse still zooms a tenth and eases in.
#! covers OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.cpp:mouseScroll OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.cpp:ShowScriptPositions OFS-lib/imgui_impl/imgui_impl_sdl.cpp OFS-lib/videoplayer/OFS_VideoplayerWindow.cpp:mouseScroll
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 30
wait 10
lanemove 30.0 50
wait 5
expect visible_time 5.00

note ten small steps in, each a tenth of a notch, land at once with no easing
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
wheel 0.1
state 01-pinched
note 5 s less a hundredth each time is 4.52 s
expect visible_time 4.52

note two fingers sideways move the playhead by a tenth of what shows per notch
wheel 0 0.5
wait 10
state 02-swiped
waituntil player_time >= 30.21 60
waituntil player_time <= 30.24 60
wheel 0 -0.5
wait 10
waituntil player_time <= 30.01 60
waituntil player_time >= 29.99 60

note Shift and a mouse wheel move through time instead of zooming
hold shift
wheel 1
let shift
wait 10
state 03-shift-wheel
expect visible_time 4.52
waituntil player_time >= 30.44 60
waituntil player_time <= 30.47 60

note a whole notch still zooms a tenth, eased in
wheel 1
wait 30
waituntil visible_time <= 4.08 120
waituntil visible_time >= 4.06 120
quit
