# Puppet recording: armed, the 3D model's handles stay up while the video
# plays, and holding one records the axes it moves until it is let go. The
# take is simplified and written over the points it covers, as one undo.
# Escape throws a take away. Add > OSR2 with twist is at 30 694, then
# 41 555, at 1600x900.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:commitTake src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:sampleTake src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:drawPuppetButton src/OFS_ScriptingMode.cpp
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 555
wait 20
expect script_axes stroke,twist,roll,pitch

note armed, the handles are up and the record button shows under the model
expect puppet_armed 0
action toggle_puppet_recording
wait 10
expect puppet_armed 1
expect pose_shown 1
shot 01-armed

note while playing, hold the tip and sway it side to side
play
wait 20
expect pose_shown 1
posehandle tip
wait 3
press left
wait 3
expect puppet_taking 1
posehandle tip 25 0
wait 15
posehandle tip -40 0
wait 15
posehandle tip 40 0
wait 15
posehandle tip -40 0
wait 15
shot 02-taking
release left
wait 5
expect puppet_taking 0
pause
wait 10
note a take of a few points on roll, nothing on pitch, which was not moved
waituntil actions:uitest.roll >= 4 5
expect actions:uitest.pitch 0
shot 03-written

note one undo takes the whole take back out
key ctrl+z
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.roll 0

note Escape during a take throws it away
play
wait 10
posehandle tip
wait 3
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 30 0
wait 15
key escape
wait 3
expect puppet_taking 0
release left
pause
wait 10
expect actions:uitest.roll 0

note pausing ends a take and writes it
seek 8
wait 10
play
wait 10
posehandle tip
wait 3
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 30 0
wait 15
posehandle tip -40 0
wait 15
pause
wait 5
expect puppet_taking 0
release left
wait 10
waituntil actions:uitest.roll >= 2 5

note disarmed, the handles go while playing
action toggle_puppet_recording
wait 5
expect puppet_armed 0
play
wait 10
expect pose_shown 0
pause

note Recording mode's 3D model input arms it too, and picking Mouse again
note disarms it. Recording is at 432 45 on the toolbar; in the Mode panel
note Mouse is at 1385 134 and 3D model at 1450 165.
click 432 45
wait 10
click 1450 165
wait 10
expect puppet_armed 1
shot 04-recording-mode
click 1385 134
wait 10
expect puppet_armed 0
quit
