# A preview video of the script, for sharing: the video with the simulator,
# a strip of the tracks and the heatmap drawn over it by ffmpeg. Started
# without the dialog, whose save picker is the system's. Four seconds of MP4
# at 30 frames a second is 120 frames; a second and a half of GIF at 20 is 30.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_PreviewExport.* src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:DrawExportFrame
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note a few strokes, so there is motion to show
hold shift
lanemove 4.0 10
tap
lanemove 4.5 90
tap
lanemove 5.0 10
tap
lanemove 5.5 90
tap
lanemove 6.0 10
tap
lanemove 6.5 90
tap
let shift
wait 5
expect actions 6

note four seconds as MP4, everything shown
preview $OUT/preview.mp4 mp4 480 3 7
wait 5
expect preview_stage rendering
waituntil preview_stage = done 3000
expect preview_frames 120/120
shot 01-done

note a short GIF of the model alone
preview $OUT/preview.gif gif 480 4 5.5 simulator
waituntil preview_stage = done 3000
expect preview_frames 30/30
quit
