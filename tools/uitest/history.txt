# Clicking an entry in the undo history undoes back to before it, and
# clicking a redo entry brings it back. History rows are at the default
# layout's 1600x900 positions; screenshots record where they really were.
#! covers src/OFS_UndoSystem.* OFS-lib/Funscript/FunscriptUndoSystem.* src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:Undo src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:Redo
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 20

note an empty history says so
shot 00-empty-history

note three points, then move one
hold shift
lanemove 4.0 10
tap
lanemove 5.0 90
tap
lanemove 6.0 10
tap
let shift
wait 5
pointmove 1
hold shift
press left
wait 2
pointmove 1 30 0
wait 2
release left
let shift
wait 5
shot 01-history
windows 01-history
expect actions 3

note click the oldest entry, three placed points, to take everything back
note rows in the default layout: Undo stack label 294, Actions moved 316, Add action 338
click 1400 338
wait 10
shot 02-undone
state 02-undone
expect actions 0
expect redo_empty 0

note the redo list is now on top; its first row redoes everything
click 1400 316
wait 10
shot 03-redone
state 03-redone
expect actions 3
quit
