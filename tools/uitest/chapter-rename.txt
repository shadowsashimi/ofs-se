# Rename chapters names every chapter from where it falls and its tempo,
# after confirming. The test media is a click every half second for a
# minute, which detection makes one chapter of at 120 BPM.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ChapterManager.* OFS-lib/state/states/ChapterState.* OFS-lib/UI/OFS_Waveform.* src/UI/OFS_ScriptPositionsOverlays.*
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note open chapters from the View menu, and detect
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 238
wait 20
click 800 332
wait 600
state 01-detected
shot 01-detected
windows 01-detected
expect chapters 1
expect chapter_name Track 1 - 120 BPM

note already named as it would be: the confirmation says so and changes nothing
click 800 389
wait 10
state 02-nothing
shot 02-nothing
expect open_popups 1
key escape
wait 10
expect open_popups 0

note give it a name of its own
click 730 451
wait 5
key ctrl+a
type My song
key enter
wait 10
expect chapter_name My song

note Rename chapters now has a change to confirm
click 800 389
wait 10
state 03-confirm
shot 03-confirm
windows 03-confirm
expect open_popups 1
expect chapter_name My song

note Cancel leaves the name alone (Rename 601,538, Cancel 753,538)
click 753 538
wait 10
expect open_popups 0
expect chapter_name My song

note Rename gives it the name it would get
click 800 389
wait 10
click 601 538
wait 10
state 04-renamed
shot 04-renamed
expect open_popups 0
expect chapter_name Track 1 - 120 BPM
quit
