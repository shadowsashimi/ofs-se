# The tip seen from three quarters (yaw 35, height 20). Across the screen
# takes the tip across the screen, which from this side is roll and pitch
# together; up the screen takes it away from the viewer, which is pitch. The
# camera is turned by Alt and a drag over the model. Add > OSR2 with twist is
# at 30 694, then 41 555, at 1600x900.
#! covers src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:poseLean src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:updatePoseHandles
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 555
wait 20
action toggle_pose_handles
wait 10
hold alt
drag 1070 420 983 353 12
let alt
wait 10
expect camera_yaw 35
expect camera_elevation 20

note across: both lean, the tip going right
posehandle tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 15 0
wait 3
posehandle tip 15 0
wait 3
posehandle tip 15 0
wait 5
waituntil pose_value:roll >= 58 5
waituntil pose_value:pitch >= 55 5
shot 01-across
release left
wait 10

note up: away from the viewer, pitch more than roll
posehandle tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 0 -30
wait 5
waituntil pose_value:pitch >= 64 5
shot 02-up
release left
wait 10
quit
