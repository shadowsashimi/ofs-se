# A second script added from Project > Add > Shortcuts gives the timeline two
# lanes, each with a header: a target tick box, an eye that hides the lane,
# and the name, which makes its script active when clicked.
#! covers OFS-lib/UI/OFS_ScriptTimeline.* src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:UpdateNewActiveScript src/OpenFunscripter.cpp:ScriptTimelineActiveScriptChanged
size 1600 900
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10

note Project > Add... > Shortcuts > twist
click 89 13
wait 5
move 90 90
wait 15
move 320 90
wait 20
click 425 178
wait 30
