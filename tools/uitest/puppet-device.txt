# A handle being dragged on the model drives a connected machine, so a pose or
# a take can be felt as it is made: here the tip, rolled with the video
# paused and every script empty, moves the roll channel of a wifi machine
# (the stand-in from tcode.txt) off its rest. Add > OSR2 with twist is at
# 30 694, then 41 555; the devices panel is as in tcode.txt.
#! server mock-tcode
#! covers src/api/OFS_DeviceLink.cpp src/UI/OFS_ScriptSimulator.cpp:PoseValueFor
size 1600 900
layout classic
wait 20
open $MEDIA
wait 120
pause
seek 5
wait 10
click 30 694
wait 10
click 41 555
wait 20
expect script_axes stroke,twist,roll,pitch
action toggle_pose_handles
wait 10

note connect to the stand-in on wifi
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 282
wait 20
expect show_devices 1
click 1034 243
wait 10
dclick 792 346
wait 3
key ctrl+a
type 127.0.0.1
key enter
wait 5
click 792 534
wait 10
click 645 576
wait 20
expect device_status 2
note close the panel again, so the model is in reach
click 266 13
wait 5
click 300 282
wait 20
expect show_devices 0
waituntil device_axis:R1 = 500 300

note roll the tip with the video paused: the machine follows the hand
posehandle tip
wait 5
press left
wait 3
posehandle tip 20 0
wait 3
posehandle tip 20 0
wait 10
waituntil device_axis:R1 >= 560 60
shot 01-rolled
release left
wait 10
quit
